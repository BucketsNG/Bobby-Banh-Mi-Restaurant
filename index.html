Our Story: Passion on a Baguette

Nguyen Banh Mi started with a simple dream: to share the authentic flavors of Vietnamese street food we grew up loving. We bake our baguettes fresh daily for that perfect crispy crust and soft interior. Our ingredients are carefully sourced, our pâté is homemade, and our meats are marinated with traditional family recipes. It's more than just a sandwich; it's a taste of home, crafted with passion and served with a smile.
